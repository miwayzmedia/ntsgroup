Privacy Policy

This page is a structural draft for launch planning and must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before publication.

Draft structure only, requires legal review before publication. Items marked [TBC] depend on company/registration details not yet confirmed.

1. Introduction

NTS Group ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, our sites and our forms, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

2. Data Controller

NTS Group is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Our registered company details are [TBC: registered company name and number], and our contact details are set out in section 14 below.

3. Personal Data We Collect

We may collect: contact details you give us directly (e.g. name, email, phone number and message content via our Contact form); details you share when telling us you're interested in working with NTS; technical and usage data collected automatically when you browse this site (such as browser type and pages visited — see our Cookie Policy for details); and, at our physical forecourt and retail sites, CCTV footage and, where applicable, correspondence relating to customer enquiries or complaints.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data to respond to your enquiries, to consider career interest you've shared with us, to operate and improve this website, to meet legal and regulatory obligations, and — at our sites — for the safety and security purposes described in section 6. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

5. Legal Basis for Processing

Depending on the activity, we rely on: your consent (e.g. optional cookies); the steps needed to respond to a request you've made of us; our legal obligations; and our legitimate interests in operating this website and keeping our sites safe (e.g. CCTV).

6. CCTV at Our Sites

CCTV operates at a number of our forecourt and retail sites for the safety of customers and staff, crime prevention and the investigation of incidents, with signage displayed where in use. Access is restricted to authorised personnel. Specific retention periods are [TBC].

7. Customer Enquiries & Complaints

Where you contact us with an enquiry or complaint, we use the information you provide only to respond to and, where relevant, investigate that matter, and to keep a record of the outcome.

8. Sharing Your Data

We may share personal data with service providers who support our systems and operations (for example website hosting), and where required by law or to protect our legal rights. We do not sell your personal data, and any third party acting on our behalf is required to keep it secure and use it only for the purpose we've instructed.

9. International Transfers

Where personal data is ever transferred outside the UK, we would put appropriate safeguards in place in line with UK GDPR requirements. Full details of any such transfers are [TBC].

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) and, where required, affected individuals, in line with our legal obligations under UK GDPR.

12. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate data; request erasure of your data; restrict or object to certain processing; request data portability; withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on it; and lodge a complaint with the ICO (ico.org.uk).

13. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected, or as required by law. Full retention periods by data category are to be confirmed internally [TBC].

14. Making a Request or Contacting Us

To exercise any of the rights above, or with any question about this policy, please get in touch via our Contact page or email info@ntsretail.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be published on this page.

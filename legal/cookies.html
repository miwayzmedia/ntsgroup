1. Introduction

This policy explains how NTS Group uses cookies and similar technologies on this website, and how you can manage your preferences. If you have any questions, please get in touch via our Contact page.

2. Who Is Collecting It?

NTS Group is responsible for the cookies set through this website. By continuing to use the site (beyond strictly necessary cookies), you consent to our use of cookies as described here, and you can change that choice at any time as set out in section 5.

3. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They're widely used to make websites work, work more efficiently, remember your preferences, and give site owners information about how the site is used.

4. Why Do We Use Cookies?

We use cookies to keep the site working properly, to remember choices you've made (like cookie preferences), and, where you've consented, to understand how the site is used and to measure the performance of our content.

4.1 Session vs Persistent Cookies

Session cookies are temporary and are deleted once you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period, or until you delete them, so the site can recognise you on a return visit.

5. Categories of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary: required for the site to function and can't be switched off.
Functional: remember your preferences, such as your cookie choices.
Analytics: help us understand how the site is used, so we can improve it.
Marketing: used to measure the performance of promotional activity.

6. Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences in three ways: through the choices you make when you first visit (accept all, reject non-essential, or customise); at any time afterwards via the Cookie Settings link in the site footer; or through your browser's own settings, which let you block or delete cookies generally.

6.1 Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Most browsers let you see what cookies are stored and manage or delete them individually, including Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Google Chrome, and Safari. General guidance on cookies is also available at allaboutcookies.org. Note that blocking all cookies may affect how parts of this site work.

7. Policy Review

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use; the current version will always be published on this page.

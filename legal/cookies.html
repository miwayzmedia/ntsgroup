COOKIES POLICY

1. PURPOSE

At NTS Group, we value your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how cookies and similar technologies are used on our website.

Cookies help our website function correctly, improve your browsing experience and provide useful information about how visitors interact with our content.

This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, why we use them and how you can manage your preferences. If you have any questions, please contact us through our Contact Us page.

2. WHO IS COLLECTING IT?

The NTS Group website represents NTS Retail Limited and NTS Motor Fuel Ltd (collectively referred to as "NTS Group", "we", "our" or "us").

Personal data collected through cookies and similar technologies is processed by the relevant NTS Group company responsible for the website activity.

We only collect and process personal information where permitted by applicable data protection laws. Non-essential cookies are used only where you have provided your consent.

For further details about how we handle personal data, please refer to our Privacy Policy.

3. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, smartphone or other device when you visit a website. They allow websites to recognise your device and remember certain information about your visit.

We may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Support essential website functions;
  • Remember your preferences;
  • Improve website performance;
  • Understand how visitors use our website.

Some cookies are necessary for the website to operate, while others are optional and require your consent.

4. WHY DO WE USE COOKIES?

We use cookies to provide a reliable and convenient browsing experience across the NTS Group website.

Depending on the features and services available, cookies may help us:

  • Ensure the website operates correctly and securely;
  • Remember your selected preferences;
  • Understand which pages are visited and how the website is used;
  • Identify opportunities to improve website content and performance;
  • Measure the effectiveness of website features and communications, where applicable.

Information collected through optional analytics or marketing cookies will only be processed where the required consent has been obtained.

5. DURATION OF COOKIES

The length of time a cookie remains on your device depends on its purpose and settings.

We may use two types of cookies:

Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that are removed when you close your browser or end your browsing session.

Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a defined period or until you delete them manually.

The duration of individual cookies may vary depending on their function and the services used on our website.

6. MANAGING COOKIES

You can control how cookies are used on your device.

Where optional cookies are used, you can accept or reject them through our website's cookie consent controls. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of some website features.

6.1 COOKIES CONSENT

We use cookies that are strictly necessary to operate our website without requesting consent where permitted by law.

For non-essential cookies, including optional analytics and marketing cookies, we request your consent before placing them on your device.

You can choose whether to accept these cookies, and you may withdraw your consent at any time through the available cookie preference controls.

6.1.1 CONSENT BANNER

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner will be displayed where non-essential cookies are used.

The banner will provide options to:

  • Accept all cookies;
  • Reject non-essential cookies;
  • Manage your cookie preferences.

Your selection will be respected unless you change your preferences or consent needs to be renewed.

6.2 COOKIES OPT-OUT

6.2.1 CONTROL VIA YOUR BROWSER SETTINGS

Most web browsers allow you to review, block or delete cookies through their settings.

You can configure your browser to reject certain cookies or notify you when websites attempt to store them.

Please note that blocking essential cookies may prevent some parts of our website from working properly.

If you use multiple devices or browsers to access the NTS Group website, you may need to adjust your cookie settings separately on each one.

For guidance on managing cookies, please visit the relevant browser support pages:

  • Microsoft Edge
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Opera
  • Google Chrome
  • Safari

You can also learn more about cookies and how to manage them at www.allaboutcookies.org.

7. POLICY REVIEW

We may update this Cookies Policy periodically to reflect changes to our website, the technologies we use or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page and will take effect from the date of publication.

Accessibility Statement

This page is a structural draft for launch planning and must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before publication.

Draft structure only, requires a full accessibility audit before publication.

1. Our Commitment

NTS Group wants this website to be usable by as many people as possible, including if you have a visual, hearing, motor or cognitive disability. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA.

2. Conformance Status

This site has been built with accessibility in mind throughout, including: full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic HTML structure and landmarks, labelled form fields with clear error messages, an alt-text architecture for images, and support for reduced-motion preferences. As a prototype, it has not yet been through a formal WCAG 2.2 AA conformance audit — we recommend a full audit (including with assistive-technology users) before this site goes live.

3. Known Limitations

We're not currently aware of specific accessibility issues on this site, but as an unaudited prototype there may be gaps we haven't identified yet — for example around colour contrast in every state, or screen-reader behaviour on more complex interactive elements like the store finder map. Please let us know if you come across anything (see section 4).

4. Feedback & Contact

If you find any part of this website difficult to use, or you need information in a different format, please tell us via our Contact page. We'll aim to respond and, where reasonably possible, put the issue right.

5. Enforcement

If you're not happy with our response, you can raise a complaint with the relevant enforcement body for accessibility of websites [TBC: the appropriate body will depend on the final site's status and audience].

6. Technical Information

This website is built as a static site with progressive-enhancement JavaScript for features like the store finder and forms, and is intended to work across current versions of major browsers, at any zoom or text-size level, and with keyboard-only or screen-reader navigation.

7. Preparation of This Statement

This statement was prepared as part of the initial build of this website and reflects our own review during development, not a third-party audit. It will be reviewed and updated once a full accessibility assessment has been carried out.
